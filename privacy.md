# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Second Genesis (the "Bot"), operated by the Second Genesis Team ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Bot.

By using Second Genesis, you acknowledge that your information may be handled as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Second Genesis, we may collect and store information including:

### Discord Information

When you interact with the Bot, we may receive information provided by Discord, which may include:

* Your Discord user ID
* Your Discord username, display name, and avatar
* Information about your interactions with the Bot

### Game Data

Second Genesis may store information associated with your game account, including:

* Character and account information
* In-game currency
* Inventory and items
* Statistics and progression
* Companies, properties, or other entities associated with your account
* Game activity and transaction history
* Other information necessary to operate the game

### Purchase Information

If you purchase a paid membership or other product associated with Second Genesis, we may receive information necessary to verify and manage your purchase.

Payment information such as your full payment card number is handled by the relevant payment provider and is not stored by Second Genesis unless explicitly stated otherwise.

### Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Bot, such as error logs, timestamps, command usage, and other diagnostic information.

## 2. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

* Provide and operate Second Genesis.
* Maintain your game account and progression.
* Process and verify memberships or other purchases.
* Provide customer support.
* Prevent cheating, abuse, fraud, and exploitation.
* Investigate bugs and technical problems.
* Improve and balance the game.
* Maintain the security and reliability of the Bot.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We will not use your information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or your consent where required.

## 3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with third-party services when reasonably necessary to operate Second Genesis. These services may include:

* Discord, which provides the platform through which the Bot operates.
* Our database provider, which stores game and account data.
* Patreon, which provides the membership platform through which Second Genesis memberships are purchased and managed. Patreon and its payment processing partners process membership payments and may provide us with limited information necessary to administer your membership and provide its associated benefits.

These providers may process information according to their own policies and terms.

We may also disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect the Bot or its users, or investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Second Genesis and fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Some information may be retained for longer where necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal obligations, or legitimate operational purposes.

When information is no longer reasonably required, we may delete or anonymise it.

## 5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no online service can guarantee absolute security, and we cannot guarantee that information will always remain completely secure.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, which may include the right to:

* Request access to information we hold about you.
* Request correction of inaccurate information.
* Request deletion of certain information.
* Request restriction of certain processing.
* Object to certain uses of your information.
* Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Some rights may be subject to legal or operational limitations.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the information provided below.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Second Genesis is operated through Discord and is not specifically directed towards children.

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws when using the Bot.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

Some third-party services used by Second Genesis may process or store information outside your country of residence.

The updated Privacy Policy will be effective from the date specified at the beginning of the updated policy.
